# Green Light Pay Wallet Connection marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance coordinates public materials. The GLP project owner must confirm any commercial agreement.

**Audience.** Solana wallet integrators. **Offer.** Help wallet developers reproduce the address-and-balance step in a mobile payment experience.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Publish an annotated provider-detection trace using fixtures and a short disconnect demonstration. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Run the mock wallet suite. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 3 independent successful fixture runs. |

### Sales test

Offer an integration checklist and a developer walkthrough. Success is one accepted paid integration assessment, independent of transaction volume. Qualify one wallet-integration support request for a future fixed-scope GLP engagement.

### Measurement and decision

Use `glp-wallet-connect-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** A mock connection does not establish native compatibility. This component neither signs nor settles a payment. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
